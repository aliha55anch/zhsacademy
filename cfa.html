Investment management · Global credential

CFA

Progressive exam preparation for candidates developing expertise in investment analysis, valuation, portfolio management, ethics, and professional judgment.

Course overview

The CFA preparation pathway supports candidates across a three-level program in which knowledge becomes progressively more analytical and applied. Lessons develop technical understanding alongside the discipline needed to work through a broad curriculum.

Preparation is organized by level, topic, and revision phase so candidates can build foundations, analyze complex cases, and integrate concepts into investment decisions.

How the levels progress

Level I · Learn & describe
Build command of foundational concepts, tools, and formulas.
Level II · Analyze & evaluate
Apply knowledge to case-based analysis and valuation.
Level III · Integrate & apply
Bring concepts together in portfolio and real-world scenarios.
Ethics throughout
Develop professional standards and ethical decision-making.

Core learning areas

Ethical and professional standards
Quantitative methods and economics
Financial statement analysis
Equity and fixed-income investments
Derivatives and alternative investments
Portfolio management and wealth planning

Who this course is for

  • University students building a pathway into investment and finance.
  • Professionals in banking, research, asset management, or advisory roles.
  • Candidates seeking structured coverage of a broad technical curriculum.
  • Career changers developing a rigorous foundation in investment analysis.
