Academic finance support · Middle East

University Finance Program

We provide personalized one-to-one tuition for university students who want to understand finance concepts, improve assessment performance, and study with greater confidence.

Course overview

This program provides one-to-one tuition for students enrolled in university finance modules across the Middle East. Each session is shaped around the learner’s course outline, current topics, assessment requirements, and areas of difficulty.

The focus is academic understanding—not completing assessed work on a student’s behalf. Sessions develop independent problem-solving, accurate methods, and the ability to communicate financial reasoning clearly.

Areas of support

Corporate finance
Capital budgeting, financing, risk, and valuation.
Financial management
Working capital, planning, and financial decisions.
Investment analysis
Returns, securities, portfolios, and market concepts.
Financial reporting
Statements, ratios, analysis, and interpretation.
Quantitative finance
Time value, probability, models, and calculations.
Assessment preparation
Revision plans, practice, and exam technique.

How support is personalized

  • Review the student’s syllabus and current learning objectives.
  • Identify conceptual gaps before moving into advanced problems.
  • Work through representative examples and practice questions.
  • Build a practical revision plan around assessment dates.
  • Adapt explanations to the university’s terminology and methods.

Who this course is for

Undergraduate and postgraduate students taking finance-related modules who need clearer explanations, consistent academic support, or focused preparation for examinations and assessments.
